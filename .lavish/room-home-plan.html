Home as a room: three walls

The home page becomes the inside of a room seen from the doorway. Left wall: the movies you like. Middle wall: Recommended for you, painted on the wall. Right wall: your watchlist. The camera starts on the middle wall and slides to the others. A hidden fourth wall closes the ring: go right past the watchlist or left past the likes and you reach a screen showing rusticit.com. Search moves into the header. Clicking a poster dollies the camera into it and lands on the movie page; Back dollies out. Pure HTML and CSS 3D, no Three.js.

1Prototype you can poke at

Click or press the arrow keys to move the camera between walls. Switch profile (the wall colour is that profile's badge tint and each profile has its own pattern), click a poster to zoom in, hover a poster and press the bookmark to put it on the watchlist wall, or switch to Phone to see the narrow layout. Poster art is stand-in gradients; the real build uses TMDB posters. Patterns are the four Hero Patterns you picked.

3Approach

Room geometry (CSS only)

A perspective wrapper holds a transform-style: preserve-3d room: three equal walls in a row. The camera is the room's translateX, driven by one variable (--i, the wall in front of you), so it slides wall to wall on desktop and phone alike. The wall in front faces you; the neighbours pivot on the edge nearest the active wall: left rotateY(34deg) with origin at its right edge, right rotateY(-34deg) with origin at its left edge, so they peek in from the sides under a dark gradient. Clicking a peeking wall, the tabs, arrow keys or a swipe move the camera. A white ceiling and a dark floor are two wide planes hinged on the top and bottom edges of the walls and moving with the room. Soft gradients on the side walls and an inset shadow on the back wall fake lighting.

.stage{--w:min(72cqw,820px);perspective:1500px;perspective-origin:50% 42%}
.room{transform-style:preserve-3d;display:grid;grid-auto-flow:column;
  grid-auto-columns:var(--w);width:calc(var(--w)*3);
  transform:translateX(calc((100cqw - var(--w))/2 - var(--i)*var(--w))) var(--zoom)}
.wall.left{transform-origin:100% 50%;transform:rotateY(34deg)}
.wall.right{transform-origin:0 50%;transform:rotateY(-34deg)}
.wall.active{transform:none}

Colour and Hero Patterns

Wall background is profile.tint. The profile's pattern (hexagons, groovy, intersecting circles, brick, copied from heropatterns.com) is saved as an SVG file and applied as a CSS mask-image over a darker mix of the tint, so the pattern recolours itself per profile and in dark mode. Brick flips this: light mortar over a terracotta wall, at 1.5x scale so the lines read as mortar. Dark mode mixes the tint toward charcoal #0e152e so a pastel wall does not glare.

Licence: Hero Patterns is CC BY 4.0, so the footer gets an attribution line. The profile gains a pattern key next to tint in server/utils/profiles.ts.

Camera zoom to the movie page

On poster click: read its rect, set the room's transform-origin to the poster centre, animate scale() plus a translate to centre it (~320 ms, Web Animations API), then router.push. The existing View Transition (movie-poster-{id}) morphs the poster into the hero. Going back: the home page mounts already scaled onto that poster and animates to identity. Needs a small useRoomCamera composable holding the last poster id and rect.

Spike first A view-transition name inside a 3D-rotated side wall may snapshot flattened. If it looks wrong, side-wall posters get a short "turn to face the wall" pre-step or a plain crossfade; back-wall posters (flat) are unaffected.

Watchlist

useWatchlist() keeps an array of movie ids in localStorage and exposes has / toggle / movies. State is read in onMounted so SSR and first hydration stay identical (no mismatch); the right wall shows its empty state for one frame. Movies resolve from the already-loaded useMovies() list, so no new API. A shared WatchlistButton (bookmark icon, aria-pressed) goes on posters and in the movie page hero. Nothing is sent to the server.

Header search

AppShell drops the <a href="https://rusticit.com"> and mounts MovieSearch in v-app-bar-title with the "Movies RusticIT" label. Behaviour stays as today: picking a result adds it to the liked list (left wall). On phones the field collapses to an icon that expands.

Phone, motion, accessibility

Phone: same ring, same rig, with the active wall at 84% of the screen width so the neighbours still peek in (swipe, tabs, or tap a neighbour). The profile switcher stays in the header on phones too.

Reduced motion: no dolly, no yaw; falls back to the current crossfade.

A11y: each wall is a labelled section, posters stay real links with the title as their name, the painted heading is a real h2 with 4.5:1 contrast on the tint, and the bookmark/remove buttons are reachable by keyboard and always visible on touch.

5Risks

Many liked movies. A profile can like dozens of films. Side walls would grow far taller than the back wall. Decided: side walls match the back wall's height and scroll inside (scrollbar hidden); a fade at the bottom edge hints there is more.
Info density drops. Cards showed title, reason and score at a glance; posters hide most of that behind hover. Touch has no hover, so the reason must also appear on the movie page.
Existing e2e tests. Several specs depend on test ids for chips, cards and the header title; they will need to be updated, not just re-run.
The rusticit.com iframe. It currently allows framing (no X-Frame-Options or frame-ancestors sent), but that is rusticit's call and can change; it also shows its own cookie banner inside the screen. Fallback if framing is ever blocked: a static screenshot of the site on the screen with a link. The iframe gets sandbox, loading=lazy and referrerpolicy=no-referrer.
3D text blur. Text on rotated walls can render soft in some browsers. Only posters and a short label sit on side walls; the long text lives on the flat back wall.
