Movie page, with the card morphing into it

Click a card below. The poster (and optionally the title) travels from the grid into the detail page, using the same browser feature the app would use. Tune the layout and timing in the panel under the prototype, then send your choices back.

This browser has no View Transitions API. The prototype still works but swaps instantly. In the app that is the intended fallback: plain navigation. Use Chrome or Edge to judge the motion.

3How it works in the app

Mechanics

  • Route app/pages/movies/[id].vue; data comes from the cached useMovies(), no new endpoint.
  • experimental.viewTransition: true in nuxt.config.ts; Nuxt wraps each navigation in document.startViewTransition, including browser back and forward.
  • Card and detail poster share view-transition-name: movie-poster-{id} and view-transition-class: poster.
  • The card becomes a real link and keeps ?liked= in the URL.

Fallbacks and guards

  • No API support (older Firefox/Safari) or prefers-reduced-motion: plain navigation, no animation.
  • The poster must be visible in the new page's first frame, so the image fade-in is skipped when the image is already cached.
  • Unknown or non-numeric id: 404 page.
  • Going back needs the card in the DOM; the cached recommendations render it immediately.

5Tests

All end to end, in tests/e2e/movie-detail.spec.ts: card opens the right page; startViewTransition is invoked and both ends carry the same transition name; Back restores ?liked= and the grid; direct URL renders on the server; bad id gives 404; reduced motion skips the transition. The animation itself cannot be asserted in Playwright, so you review it by eye, like here.
